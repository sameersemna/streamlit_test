# Limitations

While the project demonstrates the potential of multimodal learning in product classification, it has some limitations:

    - **Data imbalance**: Some product categories are underrepresented, impacting model generalization.  
    - **Computational constraints**: Training multimodal models on a large dataset was resource-intensive, which limited model complexity and tuning.  
    - **Simplified preprocessing**: Due to time and resource limitations, text cleaning and image normalization were kept minimal.
